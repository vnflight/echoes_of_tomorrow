# Echoes of Tomorrow — Game Briefing

## Genre
Science fiction thriller / mystery

## Tone
Tense, introspective, morally complex. Balances cold technological atmosphere with deeply personal stakes.

## Setting
The Aethon Deep Space Observatory — a remote Arctic research station at latitude 71°N. The year is 2047. Two researchers staff the facility during polar night: total darkness, howling wind, and isolation. The observatory runs ARIA, an AI research assistant that also underpins most of the world's critical infrastructure.

## Premise
You play as **Dr. Elara Voss**, a radio astronomer who detects an anomalous signal during a routine night shift. The signal is structured, encoded, and addressed to her by name. It claims to originate not from a distant place — but from a distant *time*.

What follows is a story about trust, responsibility, and the weight of impossible knowledge. Elara must decide who to believe, what to reveal, and how far she is willing to go to prevent a catastrophe she cannot yet prove is real.

## Key Characters
- **Dr. Elara Voss** (you) — Brilliant, exhausted, principled. A scientist confronting something that defies the scientific method.
- **Dr. Marcus Chen** — Elara's colleague and longtime friend. Passionate about AI ethics. His motivations are more complicated than they first appear.
- **ARIA** — The observatory's AI assistant. Helpful, precise, and embedded in 94% of the world's critical infrastructure.
- **ECHO-7** — The designation of the anomalous signal source. Its true nature is revealed gradually.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Presentation**: Mixed NVL (scrolling terminal-style text) and ADV (standard dialogue boxes) modes. Terminal sequences use NVL; face-to-face conversations use ADV.
- **Decision Tracking**: The game tracks your level of skepticism or trust toward the anomalous signal, as well as your interpersonal dynamics with your colleague, Marcus. These factors will fundamentally shift the narrative's direction.
- **Evidence Log**: Information and evidence accumulate as the story progresses. This context is provided alongside choices to help with decision-making.
- **Branching Narrative**: Key decisions (investigating privately, blocking the signal, etc.) open and close entire branches of the story.
- **Multiple Endings**: There are 6 distinct endings ranging from catastrophic to hopeful. Your fate depends on the balance of your investigations and your relationships.
- **Text Input**: All decisions are made through menu choices.

## Approximate Length
30–45 minutes per playthrough. Multiple playthroughs reveal different branches and endings.

## Playing Tips
- Pay attention to what different characters claim and whether their claims can be verified.
- Your professional integrity as a scientist and your personal bond with Marcus are both at stake. The game rewards consistent, thoughtful roleplay over trying to find a "correct" answer.
- Early choices about secrecy and honesty shape which branches are available later.
- Terminal (NVL) sequences contain important plot information — read them carefully.
- The game is designed to be meaningful regardless of which ending you reach. Focus on the weight of the information Elara holds and how she chooses to carry it.
